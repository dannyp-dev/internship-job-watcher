# Direct company coverage

Updated October 21, 2026. These 182 companies have configured direct feeds. The broader planning roster has 301 names; only 156 of those names currently have direct feeds. Aggregate coverage is supplemental and does not establish direct monitoring.

A company does not need an open internship to be monitored. The 48 companies added in this update include 33 with no matching internship at verification. Healthy empty searches remain enabled. Existing matching URLs captured at onboarding are recorded separately, so a new URL appearing before the first cloud check is still eligible for an alert.

The H&R Block feed covers its corporate Jibe portal. The Siemens feed covers Digital Industries Software's US postings, not every Siemens division. All direct feeds use the existing 15-minute schedule and 10 AM to midnight Eastern notification window; discovery is polling, not instantaneous push delivery.
